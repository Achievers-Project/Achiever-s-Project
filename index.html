History of RBI

The Reserve Bank of India was established on April 1, 1935, under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. It was nationalized in 1949 post-independence, becoming the cornerstone of India’s financial system.

It plays a crucial role in managing currency, supervising banks, and controlling inflation to foster economic stability and growth in India.

Mission & Vision

The RBI’s mission is to maintain monetary stability and ensure adequate flow of credit to productive sectors. Its vision includes:

  • Price stability while supporting growth.
  • Development of a safe, sound, and efficient financial system.
  • Fostering financial inclusion and digital innovation.
  • Ensuring the integrity and trust of India’s currency system.

RBI Monetary Policy & Core Functions

The RBI formulates and implements India’s monetary policy to ensure price stability and adequate flow of credit to productive sectors. It performs several critical roles that shape the economy’s direction.

🪙 Monetary Policy

Controls money supply, interest rates, and credit flow to stabilize prices and foster growth.

🏦 Regulator of Banks

Supervises and regulates commercial and cooperative banks ensuring financial soundness.

💹 Foreign Exchange Management

Manages the Foreign Exchange Market to maintain currency stability.

💰 Issuer of Currency

Exclusive authority to issue Indian currency, maintaining trust and preventing counterfeiting.

📊 Developmental Role

Promotes financial inclusion, digital banking, and supports economic development initiatives.

Currency Management & Security Features

The RBI is solely responsible for the issue and management of Indian currency. It ensures clean, authentic notes and constantly upgrades security features.

₹500 Note

Security Features:

  • Watermark of Mahatma Gandhi
  • Transparent security thread
  • Micro-lettering “RBI” & “500”
  • Color shift security ink

₹2000 Note

Security Features:

  • See-through register with denominational numeral
  • Latent image visible when tilted
  • Intaglio printing for tactile feel
  • Optically variable ink

₹100 Note

Security Features:

  • Windowed security thread
  • Color-shifting numeral
  • RBI microprint design
  • Raised printing for accessibility
