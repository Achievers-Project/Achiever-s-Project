Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is the central bank of India, established on April 1, 1935. It manages currency, monetary policy, and financial stability.

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

SEBI regulates India’s capital markets, protects investors, and ensures market transparency.

Additional Information

RBI focuses on monetary policy and banking, SEBI focuses on capital markets and investor protection.
